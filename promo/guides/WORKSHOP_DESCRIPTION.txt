[h1]New version 1.7: works with the latest Caves of Qud (2.0.211.56)[/h1]
[b]A big code cleanup and a clearer, easier-to-read popup. Sorry the mod didn't work for a while.[/b]

Look at anyone whose death or Water Ritual changes your reputation, and the popup shows each faction involved, how much it matters, and what a ritual or a kill would do.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ReynkeDeVos/CoQ_MOD_ReputationAssistant/main/promo/assets/promo_v1.7.gif[/img]

[list]
[*]The line under the title lists the factions a kill would hurt, most important first: it lowers your reputation there and leaves it below the target.
[*]The tag is the faction's priority, from 0 to 6. Try to stay at or above the target.
[*]Green reaches the target or moves toward it. Red leaves you below it.
[*]Kills show the worst roll. Water Ritual numbers are for a plain ritual.
[/list]

[b]Spoilers:[/b] the priorities come from A-F-F-I-N-E's [url=https://www.qudzoo.com/advice/reputation]reputation guide[/url] and give away which factions matter later.

Change priorities, targets and the layout under [b]Mod: Reputation Assistant[/b] in the options. It works with villages and with factions from other mods or DLC, and it's safe to add or remove mid-game.

It pairs well with [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3507307896]You Spot a Legendary![/url] by Cattlesquat, which tells you when a legendary shows up.

Source on [url=https://github.com/ReynkeDeVos/CoQ_MOD_ReputationAssistant]GitHub[/url].

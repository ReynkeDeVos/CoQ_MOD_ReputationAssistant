I got tired of tracking my reputation across dozens of factions by digging through the reputation list before deciding whether to Water Ritual, fight, or flee.

Reputation Assistant adds a readout directly to the creature inspection popup for any creature that participates in the reputation system (legendaries, named characters, anyone with the "GivesRep" part).

[h1]SPOILER WARNING[/h1]

[b]Do not use this for the first few playthroughs![/b] The priority/target guidance is based on a heavily opinionated list and contains spoilers.

[h1]What It Shows[/h1]

For each faction associated with the creature:

[code]
  [5/6 High Priority] Insects
    Rep: -100  Target: -249
    WR +50 = -50   Kill -50 = -150
[/code]

Priority tiers and target values come from A-F-F-I-N-E's [url=https://www.qudzoo.com/advice/reputation]qudzoo.com reputation guide[/url].

[b]Line 1[/b] — Priority tier (0–6, color-coded by urgency) and faction name.

[b]Line 2[/b] — Your current reputation (green if at/above target, red if below) and the strategic target — the minimum reputation threshold you want to stay above.

[b]Line 3[/b] — What happens if you do the Water Ritual or Kill. The resulting reputation is green if it stays at or above the target, and red if it drops below the target or moves further away from it.

[h1]Highly Recommended Companion Mod[/h1]

This mod helps you decide what to do once you find someone important. To help you [i]to be aware[/i] of legendaries in the first place, install [b][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3507307896]You Spot a Legendary![/url][/b] by Cattlesquat (announces legendaries and auto-journals them).

[h1]Customizable[/h1]

All settings are in the in-game options menu under [b]Mod: Reputation Assistant[/b]:

• Enable/disable the mod
• Toggle WR/Kill outcome display
• Compact layout option (single line per faction)
• Show character's own faction(s) next to its name (default: off)

Defaults come from qudzoo.com, but every player disagrees on which factions matter most. Tune them to your playstyle directly from the options menu.

• In the options menu, select any faction to override its priority and target reputation.

[h1]Details[/h1]

• Works with new and existing saves
• Safe to add or remove at any time — stores no persistent data
• Only appends text to the look popup (doesn't change gameplay)
